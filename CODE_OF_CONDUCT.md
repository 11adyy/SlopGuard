# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all contributors, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge that participation in the SlopGuard community will be a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to creating a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Attributing credit and acknowledging contributions

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind
- Offensive comments related to gender, sexual orientation, disability, mental illness, neuroatypicality, physical appearance, body size, race, or religion
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Deliberate misgendering or use of "dead" or rejected names
- Threats of violence or violent language
- Incitement of violence towards any individual or group
- Sustained disruption of community spaces (online or in-person)
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. They will take fair and appropriate corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to:

- Remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct
- Temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior violates this Code of Conduct

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- All community spaces (GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, email communications)
- Official project representations or communications (social media, etc.)
- Public spaces when an individual is representing or speaking about the SlopGuard project

## Reporting and Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the community leaders by emailing:

📧 **github@11adyy.dev**

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. All community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

### Response Process

1. **Initial contact:** You will receive a response within 48 hours
2. **Investigation:** We will gather information and assess the situation
3. **Resolution:** Appropriate action will be taken, which may include:
   - A private conversation to clarify expectations
   - A written warning
   - Temporary or permanent removal from community spaces
4. **Follow-up:** We may check in with you after a resolution to ensure the issue is resolved
